Draws every uncertain assumption from its range to show how often the program breaks even.

How it works. Annual earnings by education = BLS median weekly earnings (full-time workers, age 25+) × 52 × (1 − unemployment rate). Extra high-school graduates gain the diploma premium from age 19 to 64; extra bachelor's completers gain the degree premium from 23 to 64, minus four years of college net price and part of the earnings they forgo while enrolled. Only the share of each earnings gap caused by the degree counts. All values are discounted to program entry (age 16) at the real discount rate. The public share is taxes on the earnings gains.

Not included: benefits beyond earnings (health, civic life, lower public spending), benefits to families, and effects on youth who would have graduated anyway. The estimate is narrow by design.
